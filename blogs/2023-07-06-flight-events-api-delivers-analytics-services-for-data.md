Our analytics services are powered by Jeppesen NavData® and Airport Mapping Database (AMDB) combined with millions of live flight position reports with worldwide coverage to derive the Flight Events. What does that mean? Flight Events describe significant changes in current flight status or events that possibly require attention. They help resolve the big challenges like incomplete or inaccurate aviation information, lacking situational awareness and insufficient insights into airline operations
