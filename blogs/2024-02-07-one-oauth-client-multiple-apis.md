In the ever-changing world of Developer Experience (DX), efficiency and simplicity are of utmost importance. It has come to our attention that many of our subscribers are not fully aware of a valuable feature on Boeing Developer Tools—the ability to utilize a single OAuth client for multiple APIs. This feature significantly streamlines their experience, eliminating the need for multiple clients and simplifying the authentication process.
